# MedDigtwin — Commercial Launch Package

## Positioning

**MedDigtwin by EmergentSoft**  
**Healthcare Operational Digital Twin**

### One-line proposition

**Simulate hospital operations before changing them in the real world.**

### Customer problem

Hospitals operate under changing demand, staffing constraints, ICU pressure, bed availability and laboratory throughput. Operational changes can be difficult to test safely in the physical environment.

### Product

MedDigtwin creates a synthetic operational representation of a healthcare environment and lets teams test scenarios before implementation.

**Observe → Model → Simulate → Anticipate → Decide**

## Current product boundary

MedDigtwin currently operates with synthetic healthcare data.

It is not a clinical decision-making system, does not diagnose patients, does not recommend treatment and does not claim clinical validation.

## AWS positioning

MedDigtwin is officially presented by EmergentSoft as its **first AWS-ready product**.

This means the product has an AWS deployment architecture and automation path. It does not by itself mean AWS endorsement, certification, AWS Marketplace publication or verified production deployment.
